# M1 compatibility and accounting experiment

This directory preserves Unison's isolated 19 November 2026 experiment for local replay. Its contracts use unrestricted-mint mocks and an intentionally incomplete agreement lifecycle. The historical Windows run is the only executed result recorded here. The [WSL replay instructions](REPLAY.md) cover the original suite, six assessment tests and the omitted-checkpoint regression.

## What the source establishes

- **Principal and identity.** The vault itself unlocks PoolManager and calls `modifyLiquidity` from its authenticated callback. Core records the caller as owner. The position lookup uses pool ID, vault, lower tick, upper tick and salt. Reranging removes old liquidity before changing ticks and incrementing salt. A removal and re-addition at the same ticks can reuse the salt in M1. Vault and trader router settle negative deltas through sync, transfer and settle, and take positive deltas to themselves. Core rejects unsettled unlocks. There is no periphery NFT or Permit2 path. [Vault and router](src/Experiment.sol) · [Pinned PoolManager](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/e50237c43811bd9b526eff40f26772152a42daba/src/PoolManager.sol)
- **Clock and admission.** Treasury activation samples the actual qualifying position. Vault changes checkpoint the old flag before core mutation and refresh from core after success. The unlock callback requires core plus the executing flag. The add hook checks core, pool, vault sender, pending operation, ticks and salt. The wrong-salt rejection test also lacks active context. On external swaps the hook checks core and pool, accrues old qualification through the clipped timestamp, then reads actual state. Stopping freezes the clock. The CREATE2 address enables only before-add and after-swap. Core skips self-initiated swap callbacks, and the separate hook exposes no swap entry point. Full removal relies on the vault checkpoint. [Original tests](test/Experiment.t.sol) · [Pinned hook dispatch](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/e50237c43811bd9b526eff40f26772152a42daba/src/libraries/Hooks.sol)
- **Payment and exit.** Anyone may claim. The escrow checkpoints, computes `min(Q × 10^18, 300 × 10^18) − paid`, updates paid and transfers to the fixed manager. A reverted transfer rolls back the call. The cap is a constant, independent of actual deposits. In the recorded timeline 200 seconds pay 200 tokens and 100 remain in escrow with no supported refund. Treasury termination revokes manager changes and allows treasury removal and withdrawal before a claim. The tests do not establish exit during a failing compensation transfer or later recovery of that claim. [Assessment tests](assessment/Assessment.t.sol)

## What changes for the candidate agreement

| Term | Archived M1 fixture | C1 proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Principal and pay | Vault receives 10²⁶ base units of each 18-decimal principal mock. Separate third 18-decimal token minted to escrow. | Exactly 2,000 A and 2,000 B, both six decimals. Separate escrow holds the same B token used for principal. |
| Service window and payment | 300 seconds from activation, normally timestamp 1000 to 1300. 300 whole tokens, exactly one per qualifying second. No fractional payment case. | 300 seconds from successful activation, 100 B = 100,000,000 units. Full-precision floor of budget × cumulative Q / original duration. Earned reserves and final refunds limited by credited funding. |
| Price, range and L | Fixed opening tick 0, seed [−120, 120), coverage from −60 to 60. Seed L = 10¹⁸, minimum 10¹⁵. Spacing 60 and fee 0.30%. | Same fixture opening price, spacing and fee. A fresh opening reference fixes the grid-rounded ±1% band, [−120, 120) at parity. Seed and minimum L = 10¹¹, maximum 2 × 10¹¹. |
| Management | Manager can change after bind, even before activation or after natural end until expressly revoked. Treasury can add or rerange even after termination. Reranges use aligned ticks within ±600. | Only bounded manager operations during the active period, with range, input, price, deadline and cooldown limits. New openings get a fresh salt. Treasury owns principal, idle funds and fees. |
| Lifecycle | Seed, mint budget, then activate in the fixture. Activation requires a qualifying position. No terms acceptance, credited deposit check, activation cutoff, cancellation or unearned-refund path. | Both parties accept current terms, full funding precedes activation, cancellation and expiry have defined rights. Stop accrual, reserve earned unpaid amounts, separately refund and receive principal even if compensation fails. |

C1 still needs the full acceptance, funding and failure-isolation tests under its own terms. [Candidate service specification](../../docs/SERVICE-SPEC.md)
